## Upcoming Courses

### CSCE 21104 / ELEG 29004 — Digital Design

**Fall 2026** · 4 credits · Required for CE, CS, and EE  
Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS), University of Arkansas

Introduction to the hardware aspects of digital computers, modern logic concepts, problem solving and design principles, and the vocabulary and philosophy of the digital world. Topics include logic gates, flip-flops, optimization, finite state machines, sequential logic design, digital systems, CAD tools, VHDL, and implementation technologies. Includes a laboratory component.

**Prerequisite.** MATH 24005, or MATH 24004 / MATH 240H4, with a grade of C or better.

**Textbook.** F. Vahid and R. Lysecky, _Digital Design with zyLabs_, zyBooks, 2024.

The course develops the ability to apply digital logic to system design and to use advanced CAD tools. Students work through combinational and sequential design, number systems and arithmetic circuits, and FPGA-based VHDL labs spanning basic gates through algorithmic state machines.

Course materials will be posted closer to the start of the semester.
