I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Virginia Tech, where I worked in the Complex Network and Security Research (CNSR) Lab under the supervision of [Dr. Wenjing Lou](https://www.cnsr.ictas.vt.edu/WJLou.html).
My research bridges **cyber-physical systems (CPS)**, **artificial intelligence (AI)**, and **cybersecurity**, driven by a vision for securing the next generation of **Embodied AI**.

I build secure and trustworthy autonomous systems by combining robust machine learning with cyber-physical systems security.

## Flagship Projects

- **CANShield**  
  **Venue:** IEEE Internet of Things Journal (2023)  
  **Problem:** Existing CAN intrusion detection methods often miss fine-grained signal-level attack behavior.  
  **Key result:** Developed a deep-learning signal-level IDS for CAN networks and validated strong detection performance across diverse attack settings.  
  [Code](https://github.com/shahriar0651/CANShield) | [Paper details]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})

- **VehiGAN**  
  **Venue:** ACM Transactions on Cyber-Physical Systems (2025)  
  **Problem:** V2X misbehavior detection systems remain vulnerable to adversarial manipulation and distribution shift.  
  **Key result:** Built a GAN-based framework that improves robustness of V2X misbehavior detection under challenging adversarial scenarios.  
  [Code](https://github.com/shahriar0651/VehiGAN) | [Paper details]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})

- **NoiSec**  
  **Venue:** ESORICS (2025)  
  **Problem:** Most defenses treat adversarial and backdoor threats separately, leaving multimodal systems exposed.  
  **Key result:** Proposed a unified noise-based defense strategy to mitigate both adversarial and backdoor attacks in a single framework.  
  [Code](https://github.com/shahriar0651/NoiSec) | [Paper details]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})

## Featured Papers

- **AION** — _ICLR 2026_  
  Shows how to detect temporal misalignment attacks in multimodal fusion, improving reliability for autonomous driving perception stacks.  
  [Paper](https://openreview.net/pdf/2231eb9399876a6360105900e945d2cae4e0c45e.pdf) | [All Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})

- **DejaVu** — _SaTML 2026_  
  Demonstrates practical temporal attack surfaces in multimodal perception and motivates robust alignment-aware defenses.  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.09095) | [All Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})

- **Let the Noise Speak (NoiSec)** — _ESORICS 2025_  
  Introduces a unified defense strategy that addresses both adversarial and backdoor threats with a single noise-centric mechanism.  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13073) | [All Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})
