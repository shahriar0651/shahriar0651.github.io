## Course Overview

**4 credits** · Required for CE, CS, and EE  
Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS), University of Arkansas

Introduction to digital hardware, modern logic concepts, and design principles. Topics include logic gates, flip-flops, optimization, finite state machines, sequential logic design, CAD tools, VHDL, and FPGA implementation. Includes a laboratory component.
